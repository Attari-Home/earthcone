From herringbone flooring to curved staircases and custom feature walls, Earth Cone's interior fit-out team handles the detail work that turns a finished shell into a finished home.

Typical interior projects include hardwood and herringbone flooring, wood and glass staircases, feature walls (TV walls, marble cladding, built-in bookshelves), bespoke wardrobes and bathroom vanities, and door and skirting installation — measured and fitted on site rather than bought off the shelf.

Earth Cone's interior crews work across villas, apartments, and commercial fit-outs throughout Dubai and the other Emirates, coordinating directly with the same construction or maintenance team already on site for the rest of the project.
