Earth Cone designs and builds custom kitchens from the cabinetry up — not flat-pack units, but cabinets, islands, and worktops measured and built for the specific room.

## What's included

- **Cabinetry** — floor units, wall units, and full-height runs built to the room's dimensions, in handleless modern fronts or classic profiled and shaker-style doors
- **Display and storage** — glazed display cabinets, deep drawer banks, pull-outs, and cabinetry carried up to ceiling height to use the full wall
- **Worktops** — solid-surface, quartz, and marble counters, with undermount sinks and cut-outs for taps and hobs
- **Splashbacks** — textured mosaic, large-format porcelain, and slab backsplashes run to the underside of the wall units
- **Islands and breakfast bars** where the room allows
- **Lighting** — under-cabinet LED strips and task lighting set into the units
- **Services** — the sockets, switches, water supply, and drainage a kitchen needs, and appliance integration, handled by the same in-house electrical and plumbing crews

## From measure to handover

Every kitchen starts with an on-site measure and a written quote covering cabinetry, worktops, services, and timeline. Cabinets are then built to those measurements, installed, levelled, and adjusted on site — worktops templated after the units are set, so the counter fits the run that was actually installed, not the one on the drawing.

Because the electrical and plumbing work is in-house, a kitchen is one job with one point of contact rather than a cabinet supplier waiting on separate trades. Earth Cone builds kitchens for villas and residences across Dubai and the wider UAE.
