Earth Cone designs and builds custom kitchens from the cabinetry up — not flat-pack units, but cabinets, islands, and worktops measured and built for the specific room.

A typical kitchen project covers cabinetry design and installation, countertops, integrated appliances, and the electrical and plumbing work a kitchen needs (sockets, lighting, water, and drainage) — coordinated as one job instead of separate trades.

Earth Cone builds kitchens for villas and residences across Dubai and the wider UAE, from an on-site measure and a written quote through to final installation and handover.
