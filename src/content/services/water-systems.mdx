Earth Cone installs and maintains the plumbing systems a UAE property depends on — rooftop water tanks, distribution manifolds, pumps, and the ablution and bathroom fixtures that connect to them.

Typical water-systems work includes rooftop tank installation and connection, manifold and pump setup, bathroom and ablution-area plumbing, and leak repairs or general maintenance on existing systems.

As with Earth Cone's other trades, water-systems work is handled by an in-house team — either as part of a larger construction or fit-out project, or as a standalone maintenance job anywhere across Dubai and the rest of the UAE.
