Earth Cone handles electrical work as part of new construction, renovation, or as a standalone maintenance call — from first-fix conduit and wiring to distribution panels and finished lighting.

Typical electrical scope includes conduit and cable first-fix during a build, distribution panel and breaker installation, ceiling and feature lighting, and socket and switch fit-out — carried out by Earth Cone's own electricians rather than a subcontracted crew.

Because electrical work is handled in-house, it can be scheduled alongside construction, interior fit-out, or kitchen work on the same project, or booked on its own for maintenance and repairs, anywhere in the seven Emirates Earth Cone serves.
